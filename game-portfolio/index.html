About the data

The simulator compares your budget with 10,960 Steam games that have at least 100 reviews. Following indie developer Yaheng, creator of LoveChoice, it estimates sales as reviews × 30 (a rule of thumb known in the industry as the Boxleiter method), with developers keeping 65% of revenue. The result shows what share of comparable games would not have earned back a budget like yours.

All 29 random events come from our interviews with independent developers. Their effects on schedule, costs and revenue are illustrative.

The charts compare Chinese indie releases on Steam (2020–2022), coded by our team, with client-game approvals published by China's NPPA. The two series cover different populations.
